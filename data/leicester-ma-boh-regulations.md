## Septic Guidance

Source: https://www.leicesterma.org/health-department

### Pool Setbacks from Septic Systems
- **20 feet** from leach fields
- **10 feet** from septic tanks

### Title 5 Inspections
Title 5 inspections are conducted by private state-certified inspectors. Reports are submitted to the Board of Health for review.

## Title 5 Application Process

Source: https://www.leicesterma.org/sites/g/files/vyhlif12146/f/uploads/title_5_application_process_101.pdf

### Step-by-Step Process:

1. **Perc Test / Soil Evaluation**
   - Submit Perc Test Application to the Board of Health office
   - Schedule a perc test date with the Health Agent
   - A Licensed Title 5 Soil Evaluator must perform the test
   - The Health Agent will be present to witness the test

2. **Septic Design / Plan**
   - Have a Licensed Title 5 Designer prepare the septic plan
   - Submit the plan to the Board of Health for review and approval
   - Plan must include all required information per Title 5

3. **Construction Permit**
   - Once the plan is approved, apply for a Construction Permit
   - Pay the required permit fee
   - System may be installed once the permit is issued

4. **Installation & Inspection**
   - All work must be done by a Licensed Installer
   - Schedule inspections with the Health Agent during construction
   - Bottom inspections required before backfilling

5. **Certificate of Compliance**
   - Upon satisfactory completion, the Health Agent issues a Certificate of Compliance
   - As-built plan must be submitted

## Summary: Leicester BOH Regulations Related to Septic/Title 5

Leicester does **not** have a formal Title 5 addendum or supplementary septic regulations. Key local provisions:

1. **No local Title 5 addendum found** - Leicester appears to follow state Title 5 (310 CMR 15.000) without formal local supplements
2. **Part of LRPHC** - Leicester Regional Public Health Coalition with Barre, Hardwick, Holden, New Braintree, and North Brookfield
3. **Pool setbacks** - 20 feet from leach fields, 10 feet from septic tanks
4. **Perc test fee** - $325 application fee
5. **Septic plan review** - $175
6. **Construction permit** - $100
7. **Local Upgrade Approval** - $50
8. **Certificate of Compliance** - $75
9. **Licensed installer required** - All septic work must be performed by a licensed installer
10. **Health Agent witnesses all perc tests** - Soil evaluator performs, Health Agent must be present
