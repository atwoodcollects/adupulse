## Septic Guidance

Source: https://www.town.canton.ma.us/978/Septic-Guidance

Title V of the State Environmental Code sets minimum requirements for the disposal of sewage on unsewered lots. Septic plans should be submitted in paper and electronic form to the Canton Board of Health office at 79 Pleasant St.

### Perc Tests and Inspections
To schedule a Perc Test or a Bottom-of-the-hole inspection, contact the BOH soil evaluator, Tim Marble at 617-827-8360. Percolation Test Witnessing and Deep Hole Observations / Groundwater Determinations are charged at **$50/hr**.

### Licensed Installer Requirement
**All work involving septic systems must be done by an installer licensed by the Canton Board of Health.** This includes but is not limited to, installation, repair, upgrade, removal and abandonment of systems. To obtain a list of licensed installers in Canton, call the Board of Health at 781-821-5021.

### ADU Guidance (2025)
Septic guidance regarding the Affordable Homes Act (AHA) of 2024 regarding Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs). Section 8 of the AHA allows the construction of ADUs by right in single-family residential zoning districts. Section 8 took effect on February 2, 2025. **Any new construction of an additional bedroom MUST COMPLY with MA DEP Title 5.**

### Title 5 Inspections
Title V requires that septic systems be inspected before the sale of the home. The inspection report must be submitted to the Board of Health office for review and approval.

## Private Well Regulations (Regulation 2.5a)

Source: https://www.town.canton.ma.us/DocumentCenter/View/681/Private-Well-Regulations-and-Application

**The Canton Board of Health shall no longer issue permits for, nor allow the construction and/or use of private water wells.** This restriction applies to both drinking water wells and irrigation wells, as they inherently draw down groundwater levels.

The regulation is promulgated pursuant to MGL Chapter 111, Section 31.

### Key Definitions:
- **Abandoned Wells:** Wells constructed but not used in two years or more
- **Drinking Water Well:** Any well containing potable water for drinking/domestic purposes
- **Irrigation Well:** Any well used for irrigation and non-potable purposes
- **Monitoring Well:** Wells solely for testing/monitoring groundwater

## Subsurface Sewage Disposal System Abandonment Policy

Source: https://www.town.canton.ma.us/DocumentCenter/View/11807

For buildings connected to the Municipal Sewer System.

Septic system abandonment must be completed in accordance with 310 CMR 15.354 of the State Environmental Code, Title 5. **Abandonment must be completed by a septic system installer licensed by the Board of Health.**

### Abandonment Procedure (per 310 CMR 15.354):

1. Within 14 days prior to discontinuance of use, the facility owner shall apply to the Canton Board of Health to abandon the existing system, citing reasons and providing a copy of the sewer connection permit.

2. Upon receipt of the Canton Board of Health's written approval, the septic tank shall be pumped of its entire contents by a licensed septage hauler.

3. The tank shall be excavated and removed from the site, OR the bottom of the tank shall be opened or ruptured after being pumped so as to prevent retainage of water and the tank shall be completely filled with clean sand or other suitable material approved in writing by the Board of Health.

### Certificate of Compliance Requirements:
1. Receipt for pumping of the septic tank and pump chamber by a licensed septage hauler
2. As-built drawings for the sewer line installation
3. Written certification by the licensed septic installer that abandonment has been completed in accordance with Title 5
4. Final single component inspection by the Board of Health

**Fee:** $30 (submitted within 30 days of abandonment)

## Summary: Canton BOH Regulations That Exceed Title 5

Canton does **not** appear to have a formal Title 5 addendum or supplementary septic regulations beyond state requirements. Key local provisions:

1. **Private wells prohibited** - Board of Health no longer issues permits for private water wells (drinking or irrigation)
2. **Licensed installer requirement** - All septic work must be performed by a Canton BOH-licensed installer
3. **Abandonment fee:** $30 for system abandonment certification
4. **Perc test witnessing fee:** $50/hour for BOH soil evaluator
5. **ADU compliance** - Any new bedroom construction for ADUs must comply with Title 5
