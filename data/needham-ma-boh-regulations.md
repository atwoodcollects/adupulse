## Septic System Installation or Removal

Source: https://www.needhamma.gov/1247/Septic-System-Installation-or-Removal

### Installation Requirements:
1. **Soil Test Application** - Homeowners must sign this form, even if a contractor completes it. Application requires distance to nearest sewer connection and a written cost estimate from the Water and Sewer/Engineering Department for sewer connection. Must indicate if site is within Zone I or Zone II wellhead protection area. All soil tests must be witnessed by a Health Department Agent.
2. **Construction Permit** - Must be obtained in person at the Public Health Division, 178 Rosemary Street. System may be installed once permit is issued, but may not be put into operation until a certificate of compliance is issued.
3. **Conservation Commission Review** - Applicants must verify whether Conservation Commission approval is needed

### System Abandonment:
1. Submit "Notification of demolition" form
2. Complete "Abandonment of septic/cesspool system and connection to sewer" form
3. Confirm Conservation Commission requirements

### Sewer Connection:
Contact Sewer Division, Department of Public Works at 500 Dedham Avenue, 781-455-7534.

## Article 9 - Aquifer Protection District Regulations

Source: https://www.needhamma.gov/DocumentCenter/View/14250

Applies to all premises within an Aquifer Protection District as established in the Needham Zoning Bylaw.

### Section 9.2 - Sewage Disposal
- 9.2.1: Disposal of sewage in lagoons is **prohibited**
- 9.2.2: Use of any solid, liquid or gaseous material for cleaning, treating, unclogging, disinfecting, or deodorizing an on-site sewage disposal system is **prohibited unless specifically approved by the Board of Health**

### Section 9.3-9.5 - Toxic/Hazardous Materials
- No new or replacement underground storage facilities allowed within Aquifer Protection District
- Strict handling, registration, and disposal requirements for toxic/hazardous materials
- All leaking tanks must be emptied within 12 hours of leak detection

## Article 12 - Rules and Regulations for Registration and Construction of Private Water Supply (Well Regulation)

Source: https://www.needhamma.gov/DocumentCenter/View/24271

Adopted September 29, 2021.

### Section 12.9 - Well Location (Minimum Setback Distances)

| Source of Contamination | Minimum Distance |
|---|---|
| Property lines | 10 feet |
| Public or private roadways | 25 feet |
| Right of way or easements | 25 feet |
| Sewer line (watertight) | 10 feet |
| Sewer line (other) | 50 feet |
| Septic pump or tank | 50 feet* |
| Privy | 100 feet* |
| Leaching field (cesspool) or drywell | 100 feet* |
| Stable, barnyard, manure storage | 100 feet |
| Aboveground fuel storage | 100 feet |
| Underground fuel storage | 100 feet |
| Surface water, wetlands | 100 feet** |
| Subsurface drains | 25 feet* |

\* Setbacks required by Title 5 septic code
\*\* Referral to Conservation Commission required if within 100 feet of surface water

### Section 12.10 - Potable Water Supply
- **No private well for potable water may be installed where municipal water supply is accessible** abutting the property
- Water must meet or exceed Massachusetts Drinking Water Regulations (310 CMR 22.00) Sections 22.05-22.08
- Testing by state certified laboratory at owner's expense
- Results must be submitted to BOH at least 7 days prior to use
- Annual coliform bacteria testing recommended

### Section 12.11 - Irrigation Wells
- For sole purpose of irrigation; shall not be connected to a dwelling
- No cross-connection with municipal supply

### Section 12.12 - Geothermal Wells
- Must be located at least 50 feet from private potable water supply wells
- Must be located at least 50 feet from potential contamination sources including septic tanks and fields
- No cross-connection with municipal water supply

## Summary: Needham BOH Regulations Related to Septic/Title 5

Needham does **not** have a formal Title 5 addendum or supplementary septic regulations. Key local provisions:

1. **Aquifer Protection District** - Prohibits sewage lagoons and restricts septic system chemical treatments within the district
2. **Private potable wells restricted** - No private potable wells where municipal water is accessible
3. **Well setbacks** - Follow Title 5 setbacks (50ft from septic tank, 100ft from leaching field)
4. **Geothermal wells** - Must be 50ft from septic components and private wells
5. **Installer licensing** - Annual permit required ($150 new, $60 renewal), biennial recertification ($90)
6. **Septic construction permit fees** - $480 (major), $120 (minor)
7. **Plan review** - $275 including one free revision
8. **Local Upgrade Approval** - $150 application fee
9. **Mandatory sewer connection assessment** - Soil test application requires distance to nearest sewer and cost estimate for connection
10. **Floor drain prohibition in Aquifer Protection District** - No floor drains may discharge to ground, leaching structures, or septic systems (Section 9.7); penalties $200-$1,000/day
11. **BOH reserves right to increase well setbacks** on a case-by-case basis (Section 12.9.2)
