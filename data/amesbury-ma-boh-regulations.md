Source: https://www.amesburyma.gov/189/Public-Health

## Summary: Amesbury BOH Regulations Related to Septic/Title 5

Amesbury does **not** appear to have a formal Title 5 addendum or supplementary septic regulations beyond state requirements. Key local provisions:

1. **No local Title 5 addendum found** - Amesbury appears to follow state Title 5 (310 CMR 15.000) without formal local supplements
2. **Online permitting required** - All applications processed through OpenGov/ViewPoint Cloud portal
3. **Minimum permit fee** - $50 minimum; fees doubled if work starts without permit
4. **Health Inspector witnesses** - Deb Ketchen handles septic-related inspections
5. **Board of Health meets** - 2nd Tuesday of each month at 6:30 PM
6. **Complaints via SeeClickFix** - Health code violation complaints filed through SeeClickFix
