## Septic System Information

Source: https://andoverma.gov/faq.aspx?TID=18

### Title 5 Inspections
- Title 5 Inspectors are licensed by the state
- Septic tank pumping companies may have staff who can perform inspections, or will provide referrals
- The Health Division may have "as built" plans showing septic system measurements relative to fixed landmarks (contact office with home address to check records)

### Inspection Validity
- **Standard validity:** 2 years from inspection date
- **Extended validity:** 3 years if the tank is pumped annually following the inspection

### Failed Inspection Options
1. **If sewer is available:** Connect to municipal sewer through a town-licensed drain layer (Health Division maintains a current list)
2. **If sewer is unavailable:** Hire a private engineer to design a new system. The Health Division maintains a mailing list of interested engineers. Obtain at least three price estimates.

## Summary: Andover BOH Regulations Related to Septic/Title 5

Andover has Sewer Regulations on file but does **not** appear to have a formal Title 5 addendum or supplementary septic regulations beyond state requirements. Key local provisions:

1. **No local Title 5 addendum found** - Andover appears to follow state Title 5 (310 CMR 15.000) without formal local supplements for septic systems
2. **Sewer Regulations on file** - Separate sewer regulations adopted by the Board of Health govern municipal sewer connections
3. **Extended Title 5 inspection validity** - 3-year validity available with annual pumping (vs. standard 2-year)
4. **Online permitting available** - Applications through ViewPoint Cloud portal
5. **Licensed drain layers required** - Town-licensed drain layers required for sewer connections
6. **Health Division maintains professional lists** - Installers, drain layers, septage haulers, and engineers
7. **Inspector availability** - Monday-Friday, 8-10 AM for field inspections
