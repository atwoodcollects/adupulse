## Chapter VI - Sewage Disposal Systems

Source: https://www.westonma.gov/216/Board-of-Health-Regulations-PDF

Approved by the Weston Board of Health on December 5, 2001. Section 1.8a amended September 18, 2002. Earlier versions dated 1992, 1996, and 1999. These regulations are a more environmentally restrictive code than required by Title V. Where the regulations adopted by the Weston Board of Health are more conservative than those of Title V, they shall govern. All new systems and upgraded systems must be designed in compliance with 310 CMR 10.03 (The Rivers Act).

### Section 1 - Permits

**1.1 Sewage Disposal Permits - New Construction**
No building permit shall be issued for the construction of any building to be used for human occupancy unless an Application for Disposal System Construction Permit has been submitted and a Disposal System Construction Permit has been issued by the Board of Health. No habitation of any structure shall be permitted until a Certificate of Compliance has been issued.

**1.2 Permits for Building Additions**
A building permit for an addition to an existing structure shall not be issued without the approval of the existing sewage disposal system or a permit from the Board of Health for alteration or reconstruction. Any existing system not designed in accordance with 310 CMR 15.100 through 15.293 (Title V) must be redesigned in accordance with 310 CMR 15.404, Maximum Feasible Compliance. Inspections for additions with no additional sewage flow must have been made within the past year.

**1.3 Time to Construct**
**Construction of subsurface sewage disposal systems shall not be allowed from December 1 through the last day of February.** The Board may allow exceptions for occupied residences posing a health or safety hazard.

**1.4 Permanent Privies**
No permanent privy shall be constructed in the Town of Weston.

**1.5 Temporary Toilet Facilities**
Chemical toilets used more than three days require a Board of Health permit. Permit life is six months.

**1.6 Disposal Works Installer's Permit**
Renewable annually. Initial applicants for pressure dosing systems must provide:
- a. Certificate of completion for a training program approved by the Board
- b. A minimum of three references from other Boards of Health regarding pressure dosing systems
- c. Demonstrated understanding of system installation requirements

The licensed installer must be physically present at all inspections required by the Board of Health.

**1.7 Variances**
The Board may vary its own regulations or Title 5, except where expressly forbidden, when strict enforcement would be unreasonable. Any variance must maintain an acceptable level of environmental protection. All requests must be in writing, citing the specific regulation that cannot be met.

**1.8 Permitting Procedure**
- All fees due before processing or inspections can be scheduled
- Full refund for soil testing if cancelled at least 7 days prior; otherwise non-refundable
- Applicant's Registered Professional Engineer or Registered Sanitarian submits Soil Test Application with fee
- Plan review within 45 calendar days from submission
- Installer shall notify the Board of Health at least one working day prior to start of construction
- A preconstruction conference may be required

**1.9 Repair Permits**
All repairs or modifications shall be reported to the Board of Health and may require a permit.

**1.11 Life of Disposal Works Construction Permit**
Single dwelling permits may be renewable if:
- Plan is still in compliance with applicable regulations
- No changes in topography, soil, or water conditions at the site

### Section 2 - Design Regulations

**2.1 Location**
A sewage disposal system shall be located on the same lot as the structure it serves.

**2.2 Plan Requirements**
All plans must be stamped and signed by a Registered Professional Engineer, Registered Sanitarian, and/or Registered Land Surveyor. Plans must include all requirements of 310 CMR 15.220 plus additional local requirements including:
- Location of all wetland resources **within 200 feet** of sewage disposal system (as defined in 310 CMR 10.00)
- All existing and proposed wells **within 200 feet** of sewage disposal system
- Any water supplies or tributaries **within 400 feet** of sewage disposal system
- Town of Weston electrical permit number if a pump is to be used

**2.3 Hydrological Survey**
A hydrological survey may be required where design flow exceeds **2,000 gallons/day** or where sewage is non-residential (research laboratories, light industry).

**2.4 Garbage Grinders**
Garbage grinders are inherently destructive to soil absorption systems. **All residential sewage disposal systems shall be designed for an increase of 150% over that required by Title V** to prolong system life in the event a garbage grinder is installed.

**2.5 Distance Requirements**
- **No part of leaching facility within 100 feet of a wetland** (as defined in M.G.L. Chapter 131, Section 40)
- **Minimum 100 feet between a well and a leaching facility**
- **No foundation wall or footing within 20 feet of a leaching facility or 10 feet of a septic tank**
- **Minimum 10 feet between all subsurface sewage disposal components and any permanent building foundation** (except building sewer)
- Additional requirements in Weston Zoning Bylaws for floodplain protection and Aquifer Protection Overlay District

**2.6 Distribution Boxes**
- Access manholes not more than 12 or less than 6 inches below finished grade
- All distribution boxes capable of withstanding H-20 loading
- All unused piping knockouts shall be filled

**2.8 Leaching Trenches**
- Ends of distribution pipes shall be capped or connected
- Minimum depth of 4 inches of clean sand in bottom of trenches to protect soil from compaction during stone placement (except in natural sandy soil)

**2.9 Piping**
- All piping shall be minimum schedule 40 PVC pipe
- Pipe crossing areas subject to vehicular traffic shall be schedule 80 PVC pipe

**2.10 Leaching Aggregate**
- **Minimum 6 inches of double washed stone beneath all distribution pipes**
- Uncrushed gravel (natural stone) preferred
- Soft aggregate (shale) that breaks down shall be rejected
- Board reserves right to require aggregate testing (bucket test or grain size analysis)

**2.11 Pump Vents**
All systems utilizing a pump shall have filters on all vents to reduce potential odors.

**2.12 Use of Fill**
- Systems in fill above natural grade shall not alter surface runoff patterns adversely affecting adjacent property
- Design plan for above-ground systems must include cross-sectional scaled drawing
- No fill shall extend beyond property boundary without a slope easement recorded on the property deed
- **Low permeability soil layer required over side slopes of elevated systems**
- In areas of **high groundwater (<2 feet below natural ground surface)**, maximum allowable wastewater loading shall not exceed **660 gallons/day per acre**
- Natural soil surfaces must be scarified before placement of fill material

**2.14 Pressure Dosing**
Minimum standards:
- Minimum distal pressure head: 2.5 feet (3.0 to 4.0 feet preferred)
- Orifice spacing not to exceed 36 inches
- Duplex pump system required
- Stepped trenches not allowed unless alternative pump cycles employed
- Leaching facility shall be vented to atmosphere
- All distribution lines individually gated for line isolation
- Pressure test required prior to use with all components in place
- Operation and maintenance plan required for owner and Board of Health

**2.15 Vent Filters**
All vents shall have a charcoal filter.

### Section 3 - Construction Regulations

**3.1 Protection of Excavations**
All excavations shall be protected when unattended. All excavations shall be immediately backfilled after inspection. Failure to comply may result in fine or loss of license.

**3.1 (duplicate) Leaching Area Protection**
Compaction of undisturbed soil by vehicle wheels is prohibited. Unprotected excavations subjected to surface water runoff shall be judged damaged by silt and not acceptable for construction. Bottom of leaching facilities shall be level.

**3.2 Septic System Modifications for Vehicular Traffic**
Systems under areas subject to vehicular traffic must withstand H-20 wheel loads. Venting required if area is paved, designed for dosing, or located 3+ feet below ground surface.

**3.3 Modification of Leaching Area During Precipitation**
Work during precipitation requires removal of minimum 4 inches of soil beneath required bottom elevation and placement of clean sand to restore elevation.

**3.4 As-Built Drawing**
Required prior to final approval. Must be prepared by design engineer using same scale as approved plan.

### Section 4 - Inspections

Required inspections (minimum):
- a. All test pits, including soil descriptions, groundwater determination, and percolation testing
- b. Leaching area excavation prior to placement of any materials
- c. All system components prior to backfilling

### Section 5 - Maintenance and Repair

**5.1 High Combined Flow Systems (>2,000 gpd)**
Owners of pressurized subsurface systems shall have pumps, alarms, and equipment inspected on a regular basis.

**5.4 Use of Alternative Treatments**
**The use of enzymes, degreasers, acids or other additives in any septic system is prohibited** without specific written authorization by the Board of Health.

**5.5 Demolition and Removal**
If a building is demolished or removed, the disposal system shall be removed or pumped and backfilled with granular soil.

### Section 6 - Subdivisions

**6.1 Residential Subdivisions**
Each lot must have sufficient groundwater and percolation test data to determine suitability for a **four-bedroom house with a garbage disposal unit**. Plan must include outline of possible sewage disposal system locations.

**6.3 Subdivision Water Supply**
Evidence of inability to hook up to municipal water system must be provided prior to review of individual private potable water supplies.

**6.4 Proposed Subdivisions with Existing Buildings**
No lot containing an existing building with a septic system may be subdivided unless the remaining lot has room for providing **100% replacement capacity** for the building. Board reserves right to require soil testing of reserve area.

### Section 7 - Project Review Fees

The Board may require applicants to pay a "project review fee" for the employment of outside consultants due to size, scale, or complexity of proposed projects.

### Construction Requirements
- **Aggregate:** Only 1½ inch double washed naturally rounded river stone free of iron particles, fines, and dust. **Crushed ledge is not acceptable.**
- **Sieve analysis required** for fill material, performed within 2 weeks of construction start, approved by Design Engineer of record
- Licensed Septic Installer must apply for construction permit
- Pre-construction meeting with Board of Health required

## Summary: Weston BOH Regulations That Exceed Title 5

Weston has a formal **Chapter VI - Sewage Disposal Systems** regulation that is explicitly more restrictive than Title 5. Key local provisions:

1. **150% leaching area increase** - All residential systems designed at 150% of Title V requirements (to accommodate potential garbage grinder use)
2. **100-foot wetland setback** - No leaching facility within 100 feet of a wetland (vs. Title 5 standard of 50 feet from bordering vegetated wetlands)
3. **100-foot well setback** - Minimum 100 feet between well and leaching facility
4. **20-foot foundation setback from leaching facility** - 10 feet from septic tank
5. **Winter construction ban** - No construction December 1 through end of February
6. **Septic additives prohibited** - Enzymes, degreasers, acids, or other additives prohibited without BOH written authorization
7. **Charcoal filters required on all vents**
8. **Pump vent filters required** - All systems with pumps must have vent filters to reduce odors
9. **Duplex pump systems required** for pressure dosing installations
10. **Aggregate restrictions** - Only 1½" double washed naturally rounded river stone; crushed ledge not acceptable
11. **Schedule 80 PVC required** for pipe under vehicular traffic areas
12. **660 gpd/acre maximum** in high groundwater areas (<2 feet below surface)
13. **Low permeability soil layer required** over side slopes of elevated systems
14. **Subdivision lots sized for 4-bedroom with garbage disposal** - Each lot must accommodate a 4BR system
15. **100% replacement capacity required** when subdividing lots with existing buildings
16. **Hydrological survey may be required** for flows >2,000 gpd or non-residential sewage
17. **Rivers Act compliance** - All new and upgraded systems must comply with 310 CMR 10.03
18. **Cambridge watershed protection** - Much of Weston within Cambridge water supply watershed requiring enhanced wetland protection
19. **As-built drawing required** by design engineer before final approval
20. **Pre-construction conference** may be required by Board of Health
