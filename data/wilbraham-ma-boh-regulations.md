## Septic - Title V Page

Source: https://www.wilbraham-ma.gov/190/Septic---Title-V

### Title V Inspection Validity
- A Title V Inspection Report is good for two years, with a potential one-year extension if pumped yearly
- All three pump records must be submitted to qualify for the third-year extension
- Certificates of Compliance issued by the Board of Health remain valid for two years from final inspection date

### System Location
Contact the Engineering Office during regular business hours for septic system location information.

### Maintenance Recommendations

**Required Practices:**
- Pump systems every two years or more frequently as needed
- Use liquid laundry and dishwasher detergent; minimize bleach use
- Spread laundry loads throughout the week rather than concentrating on single days
- Take quick showers and repair leaking fixtures promptly
- Use white toilet paper without dyes or additives

**Prohibited Activities:**
- Do not dispose of grease, paint, harsh chemicals, or drain cleaners in drains
- Do not add septic system additives
- Do not connect sump pumps to drain systems; direct them to yard drainage
- Do not use toilets for waste disposal (no wipes, tissues, diapers, sanitary products)
- Do not drive vehicles or heavy equipment over system
- Do not install garbage disposals or water conditioners without system design accommodation

**Contact:** Call (413) 596-2800, Ext. 208 during regular business hours for septic system concerns.

## Private Hauler Regulations (Effective June 8, 2020)

Source: https://wilbraham-ma.gov/DocumentCenter/View/5377/Final-Wilbraham-Private-Hauler-Regs-6-8-20

Rules and Regulations for Removal, Transport, and Disposal of Solid Waste, Recyclables, Grease, Septage, Wastewater, Offal and Other Offensive Materials.

Adopted by the Wilbraham Board of Selectmen/Board of Health on 6/8/20.

### Section 10 - Additional Requirements for Septage Haulers

In addition to a Private Hauler Permit, the Board of Health requires a **Septage Hauler's Permit** and inspection prior to collecting septage from any septic tank in Town.

During an inspection all equipment must be clearly marked and identified with markings indicating the capacity of the tank. Trucks must have gauges that are cleaned and calibrated so that exact volume is easily determined.

**Permitted Haulers must maintain reports for 1 year** detailing the cleaning of septage tanks along with a statement attesting to the accuracy of the information contained in the records. Records must be submitted to the Town within 30 days of pumping. Records shall include:

1. Estimated amount of septage removed
2. Any defects in the septage tank
3. Date of pump-out
4. Capacity of septage tank
5. Disposal location

### Section 11 - Grease/Wastewater Hauler Requirements

In addition to a Private Hauler Permit, the Board of Health requires a **Grease Hauler Permit** and inspection prior to collecting grease or wastewater from any tank in Town.

### Enforcement and Penalties

- First offense: Written Warning
- Second offense: One month permit suspension and $300 fine
- Third offense: One year permit suspension and $600 fine
- Subsequent offenses: Subject to permanent revocation of permit and fine up to $1,000

Each day of failure to comply constitutes a separate violation.

## Summary: Wilbraham BOH Regulations Related to Septic/Title 5

Wilbraham does **not** have a formal Title 5 addendum or supplementary septic regulations. Key local provisions:

1. **No local Title 5 addendum found** - Wilbraham appears to follow state Title 5 (310 CMR 15.000) without formal local supplements
2. **Select Board serves as Board of Health** - Part of Eastern Hampden Shared Public Health Services (EHSPHS) with Hampden, Longmeadow, and Monson
3. **Septage hauler permit required** - Separate from general private hauler permit; inspection required before collecting septage
4. **Hauler record-keeping** - Septage haulers must maintain records for 1 year and submit to Town within 30 days of pumping
5. **Title V inspection validity** - 2 years standard, with potential 1-year extension if pumped yearly (3 pump records required)
6. **Septic system additives prohibited** - Per maintenance guidance
