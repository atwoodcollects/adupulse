## Septic Permitting Information

Source: https://www.miltonma.gov/1107/Septic-and-Dumpster-Permitting-Informati

### Online Permitting
Milton uses Permit Eyes Software for septic applications: https://permiteyes.us/milton/loginuser.php

### Septic Permit Guide
A comprehensive guide for engineers and installers covering step-by-step instructions on septic repairs and construction is available from the Health Department.

Source: https://miltonma.gov/DocumentCenter/View/5595/Septic-Permit-Guide-for-Engineers-and-Installers

### Cesspools
Milton adheres to Massachusetts State code prohibiting cesspools. Cesspool systems cannot pass Title 5 inspections and will not be approved.

### Approved Service Providers
Milton maintains lists of:
- Septage/Offal Haulers
- Septic System Installers (DSI)
- Engineers who have worked in Milton
- Permitted Dumpster operators

## Permit Fees

Source: https://www.miltonma.gov/265/Fee-Schedule

| Permit/Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| Disposal Works Installer License (DSI) | $60 |
| Percolation Test / Soil Evaluation | $60/hour (minimum 1 hour) |
| Trench Permit | $60 |
| Septic Permit (new construction) | $150 |
| Septic Permit (repair/existing) | $75 |
| Septic Plan Review (initial) | $400 |
| Reinspection | $100 |

### Fee Policies
- All permit fees are **non-refundable**
- Late renewal penalty: **50% increase** over original fee
- $100 charged for each reinspection required

## Summary: Milton BOH Regulations Related to Septic/Title 5

Milton has a Title 5/Septic Systems regulation document on file. Key local provisions:

1. **Title 5 regulations document on file** - Milton maintains a septic systems/Title 5 regulation document (PDF available from town website)
2. **Online permitting required** - All septic applications processed through Permit Eyes Software
3. **Cesspool prohibition enforced** - Cesspools cannot pass Title 5 inspections
4. **Plan review fee** - $400 for initial septic plan review
5. **New construction septic permit** - $150
6. **Repair septic permit** - $75
7. **Soil evaluation fee** - $60/hour (minimum 1 hour)
8. **Reinspection fee** - $100 per reinspection
9. **Late renewal penalty** - 50% surcharge on late permit renewals
10. **Licensed installer required** - DSI license ($60) required for all septic work
